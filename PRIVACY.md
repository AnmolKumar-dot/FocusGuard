# FocusGuard privacy policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

FocusGuard is a free, offline study-focus app. **It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers.** The developer cannot see anything you do in the app.

## What stays on your phone
Everything: your settings, schedules, focus sessions, stats, survey answers, blocked apps and sites, silenced-notification list and backups. It is stored in the app's private storage and is removed when you uninstall the app. A backup file is only created when you choose "Save a backup", and you decide where it goes.

## Permissions and why
| Permission | What it is used for |
| --- | --- |
| **Accessibility service** | Reads which app and screen is open (and a browser's address bar or a YouTube video's channel name) while blocking is on, so it can block apps, websites, Reels and Shorts. The information is checked on the spot and not recorded, stored or sent anywhere. |
| **Usage access** | Reads how long each app was open, to show screen time and enforce daily limits. Stays on the phone. |
| **Notification access** (optional) | Hides notifications during focus and keeps a short list so you can read them afterwards. The list is stored only on the phone and you can clear it. |
| **Post notifications / foreground service** (optional) | Shows the permanent usage and focus notification. |
| **Device admin** (optional) | Has no powers. It only makes the app harder to uninstall by accident. |
| **Run at start-up** | Brings the permanent notification and widget back after a restart. |
| **Internet** | Only used by the study-channel search: the text you type goes to YouTube, and channel logos are loaded. If you save your own YouTube API key, it is sent to Google with those searches. Nothing else uses the internet. |

## Third parties
The channel search talks to YouTube / Google. Their privacy policies apply to those requests. FocusGuard is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, Google or Instagram.

## Children
FocusGuard collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this repository.
